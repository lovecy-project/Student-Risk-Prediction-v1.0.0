Understanding burnout

About this project

What is academic burnout?

Academic burnout can involve chronic exhaustion, reduced motivation, difficulty concentrating, and feeling overwhelmed by study demands over time.

Common signs

Persistent tiredness, reduced focus, irritability, procrastination, poor sleep, and feeling detached from studies are common warning signs.

Project purpose

This project is built to help students reflect on patterns related to overload and wellness in a simple, supportive, and educational way.

How the score is calculated

Each answer contributes to a weighted score based on indicators like sleep, study load, stress, motivation, breaks, satisfaction, and workload balance. The result is then normalized to a 0–100 estimate.

Limitations

This is a frontend-only rule-based model, not a clinically validated diagnostic tool. It does not replace a professional assessment and cannot predict outcomes with medical certainty.

Getting support

If you feel persistently exhausted or distressed, speak with a trusted person, a college counselor, or a qualified mental health professional for support.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a real medical diagnosis?

No. It is an educational estimate based on a transparent scoring model and is not intended to diagnose illness or replace professional care.

Where is my data stored?

All completed assessments and planner items are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. Clearing browser data will remove saved entries.

Do I need to provide personal information?

No. The tool does not require names, email addresses, or other personal identifiers.

Can I print or save a report?

Yes. Use the Download Report action to open the browser print dialog and save the page as PDF.

Privacy notice

Your assessment answers, history, and planner data are stored only in your browser and are not sent to external servers. This tool is for reflective, educational use and should not be treated as a secure medical record.
